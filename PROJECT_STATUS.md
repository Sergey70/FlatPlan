## Limits

The file is authoritative for this revision and supersedes the earlier photographed passport. It does not prove real-world construction measurements. Furniture detail geometry, materials and missing heights are illustrative. Source door leaves retain their original displayed angle until an explicit mechanism is configured; configurable hinge/slide poses feed 2D/3D and actual-pose checks, while the full door sweep remains a separate advisory check. Frame profiles and furniture mechanisms are illustrative. Full-height door/window objects hide with the cutaway walls. Rooms, walls and furniture remain independent; moving walls does not regenerate floors. TOOLS-015 offers advisory projected collision/door/clearance/gap checks with height filtering and 5 mm contact tolerance; it does not certify building-code compliance or full navigation routes. Electrical points, fixture groups, schematic sheets and manual material costs are supported; cable/plumbing routes and engineering sizing are absent. Current-scene PNGs are generated on demand; saved photorealistic gallery images are not regenerated. No cloud sync. Mobile QA uses Chromium touch emulation, not physical devices.
